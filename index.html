Sweet-corn dhokla is cut only when you ask for it. Fresh coriander and coconut go on at the counter, not in the kitchen.

Mustard seeds, curry leaves and green chilli, crackled in hot oil and poured over the top. The signature sprinkle that made a parking-lot stall a brand.

Our story

Started by a father.
Carried by his children.

In 1995 Kanaiyalal Pandav began selling dhokla from a parking area in Surat. In 2015 his son Jinesh and daughter Priyanka joined, and the counter grew from farsan to full meals by the kilo. Today the family runs Radhe Dhokla with one purpose: give families their evenings back.
