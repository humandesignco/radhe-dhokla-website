1995

A stall in a parking area, Surat.

Kanaiyalal Pandav starts with one steamer and one product: dhokla. The tempering sprinkle he finishes each plate with becomes the thing people remember.

2015

The kilo counter.

Jinesh and Priyanka Pandav join. Sabzi, Chinese and pav bhaji arrive — all sold by weight. A snack stall becomes the way Surat feeds its guests.

×6

Six outlets across Surat.

Nanpura, Ring Road, Katargam, Adajan, Vesu and City Light. The Surat outlets alone go on to earn more than 16,000 Google reviews.

2023

Mumbai, and Sevanand.

The first outlets outside Gujarat open in Mumbai. The same year, Sevanand launches so anyone can book hot food for the needy through Radhe.

2026

18 outlets, six cities.

Navsari, Vadodara, Anand, Ahmedabad and five Mumbai neighbourhoods. The company moves toward a listed future as Radhe Dhokla Retail.
