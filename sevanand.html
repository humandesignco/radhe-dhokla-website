The joy of serving.
Made simple.

Sevanand connects people who want to feed the hungry with the people who need a hot meal. You book it. We cook it, pack it and serve it — fresh, the same day.

Why we started it

A bridge between
donors and the needy.

Feeding people is one of the oldest Gujarati traditions. But doing it well — fresh food, served hot, at the right place — takes a kitchen. Radhe already has one in every city. In February 2023 we opened it up: anyone can now sponsor a day of meals, and our team does the rest.

“We are giving one person an opportunity to feed the hungry every day. It is a matter of great pride for us to serve as a bridge between donors and the needy.”
— Kanaiyalal Pandav, Founder
How it works

Three steps. One good day.

Any individual, family or business can take part — a birthday, an anniversary, a company milestone, or simply a Tuesday.

01

Choose a Sevanand outlet

Sevanand currently runs from our Adajan outlet in Surat and our Kandivali outlet in Mumbai. More outlets are being added.

02

Book the meals

Tell us how many people you want to feed and on which day. Khaman, dhokla, patra and full meals — fresh, hot and nutritious.

03

We cook, pack and serve

Our team prepares the food that morning, packs it hot and serves it to those who need it. You can be there, or we can send you a note when it is done.

Book a Sevanand day

Pick an outlet and message us.

Each message opens a ready-made WhatsApp note to the Sevanand coordinator at that outlet. They will confirm the date, the number of meals and the amount.
