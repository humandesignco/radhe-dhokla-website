A family business,
moving toward the market.

Radhe Dhokla's promoters have taken a majority stake in BSE-listed Shantai Industries Limited, and the board has proposed renaming the company Radhe Dhokla Retail Limited and adding food processing, packaging and food-service operations to its charter. Official filings are published on the BSE; this page carries the plain-language version.
